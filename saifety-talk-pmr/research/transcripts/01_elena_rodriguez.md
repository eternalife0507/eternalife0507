## Section 2: Core Assessment

**Interviewer (C1):** Tell me about the last time someone on your crew or site noticed something unsafe and *didn't* formally write it up. What was going on?

**Elena:** Well, the hole Thursday. That's one. Nobody wrote it up. Maybe my text to my GF counts, I don't know. There's no form with it.

A better one is probably two weeks ago. My journeyman, older guy, been doing this longer than me. He's up on the lift working tray, and the mechanical guys have a chain fall rigged off a beam right next to us, hanging a section of duct. He's looking at the rigging and it's a nylon sling choked around a beam flange with no softener, on a sharp edge. He comes down and goes "that sling's gonna cut." He didn't tell them. He told me. And he told me like, "not our problem, but I'm not working under that."

**Interviewer (C1 probe):** Why didn't he, or anyone, formally write it up?

**Elena:** Because it's mechanical's rigging. If he writes it up, it goes to the GC, the GC goes to mechanical's safety guy, mechanical's safety guy goes to their foreman, and their foreman comes to find out who wrote it. What we did instead: I walked over to the mechanical foreman, the guy I get along with. I said, "Hey, your sling's on a sharp edge over my lift." He looked, said "yeah, okay," and they put a softener on it within the hour. Done. No paper. Honestly that's the best way it can go. The fastest. But nobody knows it happened. If that guy wasn't there, if it was the other mechanical foreman, it'd have been a different conversation.

**Interviewer (C1 probe):** What would have happened to your journeyman if he had written it up?

**Interviewer (C4):** What has your site or company tried in the past to get more people to report — cards, quotas, rewards, anything else? What happened to it after the first few weeks?

**Interviewer (C4 probe):** Why did those fade?

You told her something, and she'd be out there within the hour. Everybody told her stuff, with no app and no raffle. She'd come find you after and say "it's done." **Interviewer (C4 probe):** What did people say about the raffle or the QR code in the break room?

**Interviewer (C5):** Describe the moment you notice a hazard mid-task: where are you, what's in your hands, what's around you, and how much time do you realistically have to tell someone?

**Elena:** Okay. Most of the time I'm either on the floor looking up at my guys on the lift, or I'm up on a lift myself. I've got gloves on, cut-resistant, sometimes leather. Hard hat, glasses, vest. How much time do I have? It depends what it is. If it's "that's going to hurt somebody right now," I don't need a phone, I'm yelling. Stop. That's instant. If it's "that's not right, somebody should fix it," the pallet, the hole, the sling, I've got maybe 20, 30 seconds before I have to get back to what I was doing. My guys are waiting on me. The lift's rented by the hour. We've got a count to hit.

**Interviewer (C5 probe):** What's the longest you'd spend before you'd "just deal with it later"?

**Elena:** Thirty seconds, tops. Past that it's end-of-shift stuff, and end of shift I'm doing headcount and tool crib and my own paperwork, and I'm tired, and I want to go home. It gets pushed to "I'll tell my GF tomorrow," and tomorrow there's something new. So, realistically, if I can't do it in the time it takes to send a text, it's not happening. **Interviewer (C5 probe):** You mentioned gloves and noise. What about phone rules?

**Elena:** Phones are supposed to be in designated areas only. Down in the sub-fab you can have it on you, but you're not supposed to be standing around on it in the work area. **Interviewer (C6):** When a report does get made, walk me through its journey from the person who saw it to the person who fixes it. Where does it slow down, get lost or go to the wrong place?

**Elena:** Okay. My guy sees it, he tells me. That's usually fast, verbal. I decide, is this a walk-over or a write-up. If it's a write-up, I take a picture, text it to my GF with a note. They look at it and decide whose it is. Then they send it to that company's safety person. That company's safety person sends it to their foreman. Their foreman has to find it, figure out where it is, and send somebody. Then somebody's got to close it in Procore, which I think the GC does.

Where does it slow down? Everywhere after my GF. The GC safety coordinator's covering a whole building with like a thousand people in it. The other company's safety person is covering three buildings. And the photo I sent: I put the column line on it, but when it gets to their foreman it's "sub-fab, J-14."
