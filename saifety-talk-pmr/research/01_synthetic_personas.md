# SAiFETY Talk — 10 Synthetic End Users (Synthetic PMR, Step 1)

> **Caveat:** Synthetic primary market research is a rapid simulation tool for stress-testing business assumptions and refining interview instruments. Because synthetic agents cannot reliably predict real-world human behavior or purchasing decisions, all findings must be validated through direct interviews with real human stakeholders.

**Grounding (from the SAiFETY Talk *Upgraded* pitch deck, Sept 2026, which has the same Step 3/5 baseline as the prior version):**
- **End User (Step 3):** Frontline foreperson on mega industrial projects. Must be *effortless and trusted*.
- **Persona (Step 5):** Elena, electrical foreperson. Top fears (persona research, est.): crew member gets hurt (25%), slow response to hazards and SIMOPS conflicts (18%), blame for reporting (12%).
- **Champion:** Site safety / EHS manager. Owns the closure record. Needs defensible, auditable, fast closure.
- **Economic buyer:** Project director / VP Construction / EHS director. Needs fewer stoppages and schedule certainty. Must be *measurable*.
- **Beachhead:** North America mega industrial (semiconductor fabs, data centers, battery/advanced mfg., energy). **Pilot:** Korean shipbuilding (multilingual subcontracted crews, Serious Accidents Punishment Act).
- **Project floor for this study:** $250M+ total installed cost.

**Spectrum:** 3 early adopters (Carlos, Priya, Jamal) · 2 pragmatists (Elena, Marcus) · 5 skeptics or gatekeepers (Dale, Tommy, Park, Robert, Nguyen, who is quiet and fearful rather than hostile). **Korea pilot weight:** 2 of 10 (Park, Nguyen).

Each profile has **30 structured characteristics** (8 demographic + 8 psychographic + 14 behavioral) plus a **narrative backstory**.
