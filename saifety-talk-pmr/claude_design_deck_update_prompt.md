You are updating an existing 15-slide investor and customer pitch deck for **SAiFETY Talk**, a BuiltNOVA platform. It is an AI-triage frontline safety reporting and closure platform for mega industrial construction (semiconductor fabs, data centers, battery plants, energy) and Korean shipbuilding. The attached PDF is the current deck (September 2026). Revise it to reflect the findings of a synthetic primary market research study (10 simulated stakeholder interviews scored against 12 key assumptions, using Bill Aulet's Disciplined Entrepreneurship). Produce a **16–17 slide** deck plus one appendix slide.

## 1. Preserve the existing design system exactly

- **Palette:** deep navy background (≈ #131a2b), white headline text, safety-orange accent for the emphasized phrase in each headline (≈ #e4531b / #ff6a2b), amber for big numbers (≈ #f2a100), muted slate for secondary text, slightly lighter navy cards with subtle borders.
- **Type:** bold geometric grotesk for headlines (Archivo-like), clean sans for body, small letter-spaced uppercase eyebrow labels (e.g. "WHO FEELS IT").
- **Chrome on every slide:** SAiFETY Talk logo top right, "A BUILTNOVA PLATFORM" bottom left with the small orange speech-bubble mark, and page number "NN / NN" bottom right. Update the total to the new slide count.
- **Headline pattern:** one sentence per slide, with the key phrase in orange (as in "It must be effortless and trusted for workers — and **measurable for buyers.**").
- **Keep the bilingual touches** (이 구역 위험 신고, 무전 · 서류 · 메신저 · 메일, 중대재해처벌법) and the "est." micro-labels on every estimate.
- Keep the calm, evidence-first tone. Avoid hype, exclamation marks and emoji.

## 2. Integrity rules (non-negotiable)

1. The research is **synthetic**: AI-simulated personas, not real customers. Every slide that uses it must carry a small tag: **"Synthetic persona study · n = 10 · pre-validation"**. Never write "customers told us", "interviews show" or "validated with customers".
2. Show synthetic quotes, if at all, only on the evidence slide. Attribute them to roles, never names (e.g. "— Union steward, synthetic interview"), and use no more than two.
3. Keep every existing "est." label. Mark new price points **[FOUNDER TO CONFIRM]** until the founder replaces them.
4. The deck's own line stays: "We don't claim safety fixes schedule. The pilot measures whether it moves."
5. Real-human validation is the next step, so show it on the timeline (slide 16).

## 3. The one-paragraph story the revised deck must tell

The problem is real and specific. Hazards get spotted first by crews, stay verbal, and nobody closes them across employer lines. What's missing isn't detection. It's **verified closure that gets back to the person who found it.** SAiFETY Talk keeps "Crew speaks up. The right owner acts. The loop closes." but now defines *closes* as *verified in the field*, protects the reporter by default, and prices per project with **unlimited reporters**. It proves value first in triage hours and time to verified close, and builds the stoppage-hour baseline jointly with the customer.
